How Far Apart Are Two Forms?

Transform one sequence into another, inspect the cheapest path, and question what the resulting distance measures.

Learning objectives
  • Compute edit distance with dynamic programming.
  • Trace an optimal alignment.
  • Explain how units and operation costs define a measure.
  • Distinguish similarity in form from similarity in meaning.

Can edit distance measure meaning?

Not by itself. Cat and bat differ by one character, but that does not make their meanings more similar than words with larger spelling distances.

General lesson: before interpreting a distance, identify its units, allowable operations, costs, and the linguistic property it is supposed to represent.
