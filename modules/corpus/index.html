Step 3

Read concordance lines

A frequency number loses the local contexts that make a word's use interpretable.

Can frequency alone establish meaning?

No. Counts identify recurring forms. Concordance lines, annotations, comparison corpora, and a sampling argument are needed to support claims about use or meaning.

Step 4

Rank neighboring words

Pointwise mutual information compares observed co-occurrence with what independent frequencies would predict.

PMI = log₂(P(x,y) ÷ [P(x)P(y)])
