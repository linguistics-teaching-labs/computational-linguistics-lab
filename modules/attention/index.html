How Attention Distributes Context

Turn raw compatibility scores into weights and observe how a model-like calculation combines information across tokens.

Learning objectives
  • Distinguish raw attention scores from normalized weights.
  • Explain the effects of temperature and masking.
  • Calculate a weighted context representation.
  • Recognize that attention weights are not complete explanations.

Adjust raw compatibility scores

Step 3

Normalize scores with softmax

Softmax turns available scores into nonnegative weights that sum to one.

For each visible token: weightᵢ = exp(scoreᵢ ÷ temperature) ÷ Σ exp(scoreⱼ ÷ temperature). “Effective tokens” is exp(entropy): a larger value indicates that weight is spread across more positions.
Step 4

Build a weighted context

Each token contributes its feature vector in proportion to its attention weight.

Does the largest attention weight explain the model?

Not by itself. A weight shows where one calculation distributes emphasis. Values, later layers, residual connections, and the chosen representation also affect an output.
