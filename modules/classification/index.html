Step 4

Audit an error or shortcut

Errors can reveal missing features, ambiguous labels, small samples, or correlations that will not survive deployment.

Feature problem

Bag-of-words features lose syntax and may mishandle negation or contrast.

Data problem

A small or unrepresentative corpus makes accidental correlations look predictive.

Evaluation problem

One accuracy number can conceal which classes and examples fail.

Does a high probability mean the model is correct?

No. It means the model strongly prefers one label under its learned counts and assumptions. Confidence can remain high under domain shift or shortcut learning.

Exit question

Which intervention would most improve the error you inspected?

Choose better features, better training data, or a clearer labeling rule—and justify the choice with evidence from the model.
