Step 3

Choose a metric for the question

Precision focuses on positive predictions; recall focuses on positive cases that should have been found.

ThresholdPrecisionRecallF1False positivesFalse negatives

Precision

Of predicted requests, how many are requests?

Recall

Of actual requests, how many were detected?

Accuracy

Of all cases, how many decisions were correct?

F1

What is the harmonic mean of precision and recall?
